What Backrow is

Backrow records your lectures, turns them into notes, and lets you ask an AI tutor about what your professor said. It is run by Liam Schnorr as a sole proprietor ("we", "us"). By using it you agree to these terms.

Your recordings are your responsibility

Only record where you are allowed to. Some professors and some schools require permission to record a class; check your syllabus or ask. You keep all rights to your recordings, transcripts and notes. We claim none.

The plan

  • Backrow costs $7 per month or $25 per semester, billed by Stripe. Both start with a 14-day free trial. Your card is taken at sign-up and charged only when the trial ends.
  • Cancel any time from Settings → Plan → Manage subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. No refunds for partial periods, except where the law says otherwise.
  • AI use has fair monthly limits (for example 60 sets of notes and 300 tutor questions a month). They reset on the 1st. A normal course load uses about a third of them.
  • Prices and limits can change; we will tell you by email at least 14 days before a price change takes effect for you.

The AI can be wrong

Notes, answers, quizzes and marks are produced by an AI model from your transcript. It is told to say when something was not covered rather than guess, but it can still mishear, miss things or get them wrong. Check anything that matters against your own notes and your professor. Backrow is a study aid, not a substitute for attending class.

Privacy — what we store and where

  • On your device: everything — audio, transcripts, notes, quizzes, cards — lives in your browser first. If you never sign in, nothing leaves your device except the AI requests below and live captions (see next point).
  • Live captions use your browser's own speech engine. In Chrome that means Google's speech service hears the audio while captions are on. "Transcribe with AI" on uploaded audio runs entirely on your device.
  • When you sign in (email + code), your transcripts, notes and recordings sync to our database and storage (Supabase, hosted in the United States) so your phone and laptop share one library. Only you can read your rows; we do not look at your lectures unless you ask us to for support.
  • AI requests (notes, tutor, quizzes, briefs, syllabus reading) are sent from our server to Google's Gemini API with the transcript needed to answer. Google processes them under its API terms and does not use paid-API data to train its models. We store how many calls you made each month and roughly how large they were — not the content.
  • Canvas: if you paste your Canvas calendar link, our server fetches that feed on your behalf and returns the events to your device. We do not store the feed.
  • Payments: Stripe handles your card. We never see the number. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and your plan status.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking pixels. We do not sell or share your data with anyone beyond the services named above, which we use only to run Backrow.

Deleting your account

Settings → Plan → Delete my account cancels any subscription and permanently removes your synced lectures, notes, audio, usage counts and account from our servers, immediately. Copies on your own devices stay until you clear the site data or use Discard/Delete in the app. Email us if you want confirmation.

Sensible limits

Do not use Backrow to record people without permission, to break your school's academic-integrity rules, or to attack the service. We can suspend accounts that do. The service is provided as is; our liability is limited to what you paid us in the last three months, to the extent the law allows.
